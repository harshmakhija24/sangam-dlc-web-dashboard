Methodology

How Learning Works on SWAYAM

A transparent, academically rigorous 5-stage pedagogical workflow designed to guarantee deep comprehension and official institutional credentials.

01 app_registration

ENROL

Register freely with your SWAYAM or Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) ID in minutes with zero course fee.

02 play_lesson

LEARN

Stream structured video lectures, read weekly study guides, and review authentic management cases.

03 assignment

PRACTISE

Complete weekly graded assignments, self-assessments, and interactive simulations for active recall.

04 forum

ENGAGE

Participate in moderated discussion forums with teaching assistants, IIMB faculty, and peer cohorts.

05 military_tech

CERTIFY

Register for the proctored exam, earn an authentic IIMB-SWAYAM certificate, and claim university credits.
