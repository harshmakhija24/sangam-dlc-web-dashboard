Frequently Asked Questions

Are Sangam courses really free? ▼

Yes! All Sangam DLC courses are completely free for higher-education faculty in India. They are funded by IIMBx and supported by IIM Bangalore.

Who can join Sangam programs? ▼

Sangam programs are open to all higher-education faculty, instructional designers, and EdTech professionals in India. Some programs are open to international educators as well.

Do I get a certificate? ▼

Yes, upon successful completion of a course you receive a certificate from IIMBx / IIM Bangalore that you can share on LinkedIn and include in your academic portfolio.

How can my institution partner with Sangam? ▼

We welcome institutional partnerships! Please email sangam@iimb.ac.in with details about your institution and the nature of collaboration you're interested in.
