Our Mission

Democratising Digital Pedagogy for Every Educator

Sangam exists to ensure every higher-education faculty member in India — regardless of their institution's size or budget — has access to world-class digital teaching training, a peer community, and open learning resources.

We believe quality teaching is a multiplier effect: one trained faculty member improves the learning outcomes of hundreds of students every semester.

Our Journey

How We Got Here

2020

Founded at IIM Bangalore

Sangam begins as a small faculty development initiative during the COVID-19 pivot to online teaching, supported by IIMBx.

2021

First National Cohort — 500 Faculty

The inaugural Digital Pedagogy Essentials cohort trains 500 faculty from 45 institutions across India.

2022

Community Platform Launch

The Sangam peer-learning platform launches with forums, resource library, and inter-institution collaboration tools.

2023

AI in Education Track Added

New AI-focused curriculum added, training 3,000+ faculty on ChatGPT, automated assessment, and AI ethics in HE.

2025

15,000 Faculty, 800 Institutions

Sangam reaches milestone of 15,000 trained faculty across 28 states and 800+ institutions — and is still growing.

Our Values

What We Stand For

01

Access for All

Every program is free. We refuse to let geography or budget be a barrier to quality faculty development.

02

Peer Power

The best learning happens between peers. We design for collaboration, not just consumption.

03

Research First

Every curriculum choice is backed by IIM Bangalore's teaching & learning research — not trends or guesswork.

04

India-Centric

Programs address the specific context of Indian HEIs — diverse student bodies, low bandwidth, and local languages.

05

Open by Default

We publish all our research, toolkits, and resources as open educational resources for the global community.

06

Continuous Growth

We hold ourselves to the same standard we ask of faculty: always learning, always improving.
