Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about our programs, support, and platform. Everything you need to know about partnering with the IIMBx digital teaching ecosystem.

Q1 What is Sangam DLC, and who is it designed for?
expand_more

Sangam DLC (Digital Learning Community) is a premier initiative backed by IIMBx. It connects educators, higher-education faculty, instructional designers, and institution leaders to exchange cutting-edge pedagogical tools, hybrid classroom strategies, and digital teaching resources.

verified Includes access to IIMBx research working papers, prompt kits, and peer networking.
Q2 Is membership in Sangam DLC restricted to IIM faculty only?
expand_more

No. While curated by IIMBx educators, membership is open to faculty members, adjunct professors, teaching assistants, and edtech practitioners across all accredited universities, business schools, and technical institutes across India and globally.

Q3 Are certificates issued upon completion of Sangam DLC programs?
expand_more

Yes! Participants completing structured Faculty Development Programs (FDPs) and certification pathways receive an official IIMBx Executive Digital Certificate verifiable on LinkedIn and national academic credentials depositories.

workspace_premium Certificates are cryptographically verifiable via QR code and comply with National Higher Education accreditation guidelines.
Q4 How do I apply for institutional sponsorship or bulk faculty enrollment?
expand_more

Institutions wishing to onboard department cohorts (minimum 5 faculty members) qualify for institutional licensing rates and dedicated LMS integration assistance.

To initiate a cohort request, contact our Academic Partnerships Liaison at partnerships.iimbx@iimb.ac.in or visit the Contact page with your institution's NIRF or accreditation details.

Q5 What payment methods are supported for course registration?
expand_more

We accept all major credit/debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, American Express), UPI, Net Banking through 50+ Indian banks, as well as institutional purchase orders (NEFT/RTGS) with GST-compliant electronic invoices.

Q6 Can I defer my program enrollment to an upcoming batch?
expand_more

Yes. Deferral requests are accommodated up to 7 calendar days before the official cohort launch date without any penalty. One deferral per enrollment is permitted within a 12-month academic cycle.

Q7 Which LMS platforms are compatible with Sangam DLC resources?
expand_more

Our templates and modules adhere strictly to SCORM 1.2 / 2004 and LTI 1.3 standards. They deploy natively in:

  • Canvas LMS by Instructure
  • Moodle (v3.9 LTS through 4.3+)
  • Blackboard Learn Ultra
  • Google Classroom and Microsoft Teams for Education
Q8 I cannot access the video masterclasses. How can I troubleshoot?
expand_more

Video masterclasses are delivered via secure CDN streaming. If playback is interrupted:

  1. Verify your browser allows third-party cookies for authenticated session tokens.
  2. Disable aggressive ad-blockers or whitelist *.iimbx.edu.in.
  3. If accessing via university campus proxy/VPN, check with your network administrator regarding WebRTC port permissions.
Q9 How do I reset my Sangam DLC faculty portal password?
expand_more

Click on Faculty Sign In on the top right, select Forgot Password, and input your registered institutional email address. An automated reset token valid for 30 minutes will be dispatched immediately.

Q10 Can I adapt the ChatGPT prompt kits and rubrics for my own syllabus?
expand_more

Yes! All prompt libraries, course syllabi, and micro-simulation templates are distributed under Creative Commons (CC BY-NC 4.0). You are encouraged to modify, localize, and use them in non-commercial university classrooms with proper attribution to Sangam DLC, IIMBx.

Q11 How can I contribute my own pedagogical artifact or research paper?
expand_more

We welcome submissions from educators worldwide! Navigate to the Resources Directory and click Submit an Artifact. Submissions are peer-reviewed by the IIMBx Academic Advisory Board within 14 business days before being indexed in the community repository.

Q12 Are the micro-simulation spreadsheets accessible on mobile devices?
expand_more

Yes. While full model customization is best performed on desktop screens using Excel or Google Sheets, the interactive decision trees and web-based calculators are fully responsive and function smoothly on tablets and smartphones.
