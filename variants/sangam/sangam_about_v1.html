About Sangam Digital Learning Community

India's premier peer-learning network for higher-education faculty — empowering educators to master digital pedagogy and AI-powered teaching.

Our Mission

Bridging the Digital Divide in Indian Higher Education

Sangam — meaning "confluence" in Sanskrit — brings together educators, instructional designers, and technology specialists from across India to collectively transform teaching for the digital age.

Founded by IIMBx (IIM Bangalore's digital learning arm), Sangam provides free, high-quality training in digital pedagogy, AI-assisted teaching, and blended learning — ensuring every faculty member in India has access to world-class teaching tools and methods.

🎯 Accessibility

All programs free & open to faculty across India

🤝 Peer Learning

Faculty-to-faculty knowledge exchange networks

Our Journey

Milestones Along the Way

From a pilot workshop to India's largest digital pedagogy community.

2020

The Beginning

IIMBx launches first digital pedagogy workshops for faculty during the shift to online education.

2022

Sangam Community Founded

Sangam DLC officially established as a dedicated platform for faculty peer learning and digital skills.

2024

10,000+ Faculty Trained

Cross the milestone of training 10,000 faculty members across 500+ institutions in India.

2026

AI in Education Push

Launch dedicated AI-powered teaching programs. Community grows to 15,000+ faculty across 28 states.
